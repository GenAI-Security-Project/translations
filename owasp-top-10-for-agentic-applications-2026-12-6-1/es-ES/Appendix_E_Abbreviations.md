# Apéndice E – Abreviaturas

JIT - Just-In-Time (credenciales o privilegios) AWS - Amazon Web Services

KMS - Key Management Service BOM - Bill of Materials

LLM - Large Language Model CDR - Content Disarm and Reconstruction
